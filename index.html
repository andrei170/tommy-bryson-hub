Highest Revenue Lever
01

The Mentorship Cohort Layer Between The YouTube Content And The Free Call

The current ladder is essentially one step deep. Free YouTube content for the 590K-subscriber audience that wants the "9-to-5 to passive income" answer at the top. The $249 Simple Budget Course on Teachable in the middle (entry-level cash-flow foundation). A Patreon community tier at the bottom, sitting as a tip jar. Everyone in the audience who wants the playbook applied to their own income stage has nowhere mid-tier to land. Two tracks fill the gap: Track A (Build The First Passive Income Line) for the 9-to-5 viewer at zero passive-income revenue (mid-four-figure range, 8 weeks, caps at 25), and Track B (Stack The Income Lines) for the operator with one income line already earning (lower-four-figure range, 6 weeks, caps at 30). Quarterly cadence on both tracks delivers a $344K direct Year-1 floor, sized conservatively against comparable personal-finance creator cohorts (Graham Stephan workshops, Codie Sanchez tiers, Ramit Sethi flagship live programs). The $249 budget course stays in place as the entry-level cash-flow foundation.

Cost of Waiting

~$70-90K per cohort cycle not collected. Each quarter without the mentorship layer is one full cohort cycle that does not exist - and the 590K audience that subscribed for the "9-to-5 to passive income" answer continues to consume free content with nowhere paid to convert mid-tier.

6 months from now -~$170-230K in cohort revenue never collected, plus the audience growth keeps building toward a list that has no place to spend mid-tier.

Fix - Two-track cohort. Track A (Build The First Passive Income Line) + Track B (Stack The Income Lines). Year-1 floor $344K direct on the cohorts alone, conservatively sized. Not investment advice.
The Diagnostic
02

The Free-Call Hook Is The Cohort Application Surface, Hidden Inside The Linktree

linktr.ee/tommybryson surfaces a "schedule a free call" hook. That hook is currently the deepest point of the funnel - and it is structurally the right surface to productise as the cohort application gate. Right now the call has no curriculum behind it, no readiness filter in front of it, no application form sized for an actual cohort cap, and no public landing page that frames the next step. The fix is short. Re-purpose the free-call hook as the cohort-application surface with a Typeform application gate (uTtFirS4), a cohort landing page that replaces the "schedule a call" link in bio, and the free-call slot preserved as the post-application consult for approved applicants who want to talk before paying.

Cost of Waiting

~$3-7K/mo in cohort fills not routed through a productised application gate - ~$40-80K/yr compounding for life of the activated funnel. The free-call traffic exists today. The form, the cap, and the cohort behind it do not.

6 months from now -~$20-40K in cohort fills never routed + the IG / YouTube bio link still terminates at a deep-funnel surface with no curriculum behind it.

Fix - Productise the free-call hook as the Typeform application gate (uTtFirS4). Cohort landing replaces the free-call link as the bio primary. Free call stays alive as the post-application consult. $40-80K Year-1 incremental routing lift.
Fast Install
03

No Email List, No Scorecard, No Lead Magnet - The 590K YouTube Audience Exits Untracked

There is no Tommy-owned email list visible. The Linktree routes warm traffic to Teachable, Patreon, the free-call hook, the merch store, and the Spotify podcast - none of which capture an email into a tracked, segmented list owned by the creator. There is no quiz, no scorecard, no PDF download, no calculator. Every YouTube subscriber who watches a long-form, gets pulled by the trajectory story, and clicks the bio link exits without entering a list that can be nurtured. A 90-second "How Close Are You To Your First Passive Income Line?" scorecard (12 questions across cash flow / foundation / first line / operator inputs, returns a 0-100 readiness score plus top-three-gaps, captures email) turns the warm-but-not-yet-buyer audience into a tracked, tagged subscriber list.

Cost of Waiting

~$2-5K/mo in nurture-driven cohort and $249-course fills not happening - ~$20-60K/yr compounding for life of the list you do not have. Every YouTube long-form is a free public service until the scorecard ships. After it ships, every long-form is a tracked lead-gen unit.

6 months from now -~$10-30K never captured + the email list is 6 months smaller than it should be when the next cohort opens.

Fix - "How Close Are You To Your First Passive Income Line?" scorecard on ScoreApp or Typeform. 8K-20K incremental Year-1 subscribers. $20-60K Year-1 lift from list-driven cohort and $249-course fills.
Compound Pickup
04

The Patreon Page Is Configured As A Tip Jar Instead Of As A Community Asset

patreon.com/tommybryson is live at the £2.50 / month tier and reads as a forgotten side surface. Re-position as the alumni / community lane for cohort grads, plus introduce a paid mid-tier (around £15 / month) for non-cohort viewers who want deeper content access without the cohort commitment. This is a slower lever - it does not move Year-1 revenue the way the cohort, the application gate, or the scorecard do. It is on the list because it compounds. The fix is a two-track Day-30 install: (a) the existing £2.50 tier stays as the alumni / community access for cohort grads, and (b) a new £15 / month paid mid-tier sits next to it for the audience that wants deeper content access without the cohort commitment.

Cost of Waiting

~$400-1.3K/mo in mid-tier Patreon revenue not collected - the $5-15K/yr compound-community line stays untracked. Every podcast guest spot, every YouTube end-screen, every bio link keeps the Patreon page reading as a tip jar rather than as a paid community surface.

12 months from now -Another year of YouTube long-forms ending with end-screens that route to a £2.50 / mo tier instead of a £15 / mo community lane.

Fix - Patreon repositioning. £15 / month paid mid-tier for non-cohort viewers. £2.50 / month tier stays as alumni / community access. $5-15K Year-1 floor. Compounds month 12 onward.
