Foundation

Copy Strategy - Tommy Bryson

Built: 2026-05-16 Frame: Voice, brand pillars, headline bank, and the framing AG1 will write to across the cohort landing, scorecard, email sequences, and the hub deliverables.

Voice DNA

Tommy's public voice across YouTube long-form, IG bio, Facebook page tagline, and the Teachable course landing:

  • Direct, declarative, operator-grounded. His own course landing reads: "I went from $1,000 / month, through a $60 / week hardship period, to $23,000 / month current income over 3 years." That is the register - first-person, real numbers, no embellishment, no guru-circuit framing.
  • Money-vocabulary, not finance-jargon. Words like flip, invest, reinvest, passive income, save, build, dividend, side income belong. Words like alpha, exposure, asset class, derivative, structured product do not. He is in the personal-finance-creator register, not the hedge-fund-analyst register.
  • 9-to-5 anchored, not Lambo-and-mansion aesthetic. The brand is built on the trajectory of the working person, not the trajectory of the already-rich operator. Copy mirrors that - the audience is in a job, the work is to get them to the first income line, then to stack them.
  • "Read a Book a Week" is the brand-credibility anchor. It signals discipline, long-term thinking, and a non-hype information diet. Copy respects that pillar - the cohort is the install version of what the books teach.
  • Founder-led, not influencer-flat. Tommy is monetising his own story, not a brand-only persona. Copy treats him as the operator of the offer, not a mascot for it.

Brand pillars (what the copy stands on)

  1. Ten years on the trajectory. The YouTube channel has been running since September 2015. The audience watched the trajectory in public over a decade. The cohort teaches what he has actually done, not what he has read about.
  2. The $1K / $60 / $23K trajectory in his own words. His own published arc - $1,000 / month, through a $60 / week hardship period, to $23,000 / month over 3 years - is the cohort's central credibility anchor. The cohort productises that arc into curriculum.
  3. 590K-subscriber audience built on the explicit positioning. "9-5 Income to Passive Income" is in his Instagram bio, on his Facebook page, on his YouTube channel-about. The audience self-selected for the offer that is now being built.
  4. Read a Book a Week. The brand-discipline pillar. The cohort is the install version of long-form learning.
  5. Real assets, real numbers, real timelines. Brand deals, dividend positions, savings rate, side-income lines, the first passive-income milestone. The income lane is concrete and earned, not aspirational.

What the cohort offer is, in one paragraph

A two-track cohort taught by Tommy Bryson for two parallel audiences. Track A is the 9-to-5 viewer at zero passive-income revenue: income audit, cash-flow stabilisation, first $1K to $5K saved-and-protected, first dividend or index-fund position, first side-income line, first "income from money" milestone. 8 weeks. Track B is the early operator with one income line already earning: line-two and line-three selection, portfolio construction across paper and cash-flow assets, tax-efficiency basics, the "$5K / mo to $15K / mo" plateau and how to break it. 6 weeks. Both tracks include weekly live calls with Tommy, weekly office hours, a private cohort room, and a final-week 1:1 review. Quarterly cadence. Pricing sits between the $249 budget course at the bottom of the ladder and bespoke 1:1 work at the top. General financial education, not personalised investment advice. Not investment advice. Results vary by capital, market, and operator execution.

Body voice rules

  • Open every section with a specific number or a specific outcome. "Ten years on the channel" reads stronger than "years of experience creating content."
  • Cite the channel, the IG bio framing, the trajectory by exact figures Tommy publicly states. Generic "experienced creator" weakens.
  • Use "income line," "save and protect," "first $1K," "passive income," "side income," "dividend position," "cash flow" generously - they are the cohort's vocabulary.
  • Avoid "passive income on autopilot," "financial freedom unlocked," "make money in your sleep," "the rich do not want you to know," "get rich quick," "set and forget" - guru-circuit cliches that read instantly fake against Tommy's voice.
  • Avoid "transform your life," "unlock your potential," "level up your wealth" - banned across every AG1 copy surface.
  • Allow "build," "stack," "compound," "save," "invest," "audit," "operator," "cadence," "playbook" - the audience knows these terms and they signal precision.

Voice rules - DO NOT USE

This list ships only inside this strategy doc. It does not appear in research, vsl, email-sequence, or any client-facing surface.

  • "Game-changer" (banned everywhere by the copy-rules)
  • "Revolutionary," "unprecedented," "next level"
  • "Transform your life," "unlock your potential" (banned)
  • "In today's fast-paced world," "navigate the complexities," "delve into" (banned)
  • "Paradigm shift," "synergy" (banned)
  • "Passive income on autopilot," "make money in your sleep," "financial freedom unlocked"
  • "The rich do not want you to know," "the secret they will not teach you," "what they hide from the average person"
  • "Guaranteed returns," "X% passive income," "$Y / month guaranteed" - regulated-niche violations
  • "Lambo," "private jet," "yacht life," "100x your money" - aesthetic that does not match Tommy's voice
  • Specific stock picks, specific crypto positions, specific real-estate addresses, specific dollar return predictions for the participant
  • Em dashes - the entire package uses spaced hyphens or sentence rebuilds

Outcomes language

Outcome-language is anchored to specific deliverables, not vague promises:

  • "By week 4, you have an income audit complete, a written cash-flow plan, and a first $1K saved-and-protected target in your brokerage or high-yield account."
  • "By week 6, you have a first dividend or index-fund position opened, a first side-income line scoped, and a written 90-day execution plan reviewed by Tommy."
  • "By week 8, you have your first passive-income line earning, a written 12-month execution plan, and a 1:1 plan review with Tommy."

Promise-language to avoid:

  • "You will hit $5K / mo in passive income in 90 days." (Promise, regulated-niche violation.)
  • "Quit your 9-to-5 by graduation." (Aspiration-bait. Off-brand.)
  • "Guaranteed $X / mo in dividends by week 8." (Income-claim, regulated-niche risk, false certainty.)

Comparison framing (against alternative offers)

The cohort competes with:

  • Free YouTube + free IG learning (the current default). The cohort beats it on speed (8 weeks of guided live work versus 12-24 months of fragmented self-study), on structure (a sequenced curriculum versus scattered tips), and on live access to the creator who actually walked the trajectory.
  • The $249 Simple Budget Course on Teachable. The cohort sits above the budget course in the ladder. The budget course teaches cash-flow foundations. The cohort takes the operator from "I budgeted my paycheque" to "I have a working first passive-income line." Same vocabulary, deeper work.
  • Generic personal-finance courses ($297-$997 evergreen video courses by Graham Stephan, Ramit Sethi, others). The cohort beats these on live access, cohort dynamics, the 9-to-5-to-passive-income specialisation, and on actually being taught by Tommy live.
  • Robo-advisor + index-fund DIY approach (the Vanguard / Betterment / Acorns route). The cohort does not replace passive investing - it teaches the operator how to layer the side-income line on top of the passive-investing foundation, which is the bottleneck for most 9-to-5 viewers.
  • 1:1 financial coaching ($200-$500 / hour rates). The cohort beats this on price-per-hour-of-value (8 weeks of structured live calls plus office hours plus cohort room costs less than 5-8 hours of 1:1). It loses to 1:1 on bespoke depth, which is by design - the cohort is the wedge between free content and bespoke 1:1, not a replacement for it.

Compliance line on landing-page copy

Anywhere the cohort touches the topic of income, dividends, side-income, investing, or passive-income outcomes:

  • Outcomes vary by capital, market, and operator execution
  • The cohort teaches general financial education and operator coaching
  • The cohort does not provide personalised investment, tax, or legal advice
  • Cohort enrolment is open to all applicants sized for the work
  • Not investment advice. Results vary by capital, market, and operator execution.

This language ships in the landing-page footer and the cohort terms. It is also the line every ad creative writes to. The investment-niche footer is mandatory on every public surface in this package - the explicit line "Not investment advice. Results vary by capital, market, and operator execution." appears on the hub, the landing, the bottleneck-analysis, the email-sequence, the vsl-preview.
