VSL Preview Script - Tommy Bryson

Built: 2026-05-16 Format: 90-second cohort intro VSL preview. Shot on Tommy. Hero of the cohort landing page once filmed. Production note: This is the short preview spec, not a full 8-minute long-form VSL. Production version of long-form ships after the first cohort cycle proves conversion. Until the production VSL is filmed, the demo landing uses a placeholder card.

Spec

  • Length: 90 seconds, hard cap at 100
  • Format: Direct-to-camera, talking-head, eye-level framing
  • Setting: Tommy in his standard YouTube long-form filming location, or a neutral indoor location with daylight through a window. Operator-grounded, not styled-pitch-deck. Not a Lambo backdrop, not a private-jet setting, not a mansion living room - the visual matches the operator-grounded voice rule, not the guru-circuit trope.
  • Wardrobe: What he would wear to film a normal YouTube long-form. T-shirt, casual shirt, hoodie. Operator-relaxed.
  • Lighting: Soft key + fill. Natural daylight is fine if controlled. Avoid hard rim light, avoid neon backdrops, avoid heavy ring-light glare.
  • Audio: Lav mic or shotgun. Quiet room. No background music.
  • Lower-third (on-screen graphic): "Tommy Bryson - @tommybryson - YouTube Since 2015 - 590K Subscribers" appears at 0:03 for 4 seconds.

Script

[0:00 - 0:08] HOOK

If you have watched my YouTube videos in the last twelve months, you have probably seen me say I built my income through content creation, paper-asset investing, and the systematic side-income lines. This is the install version of the playbook.

[0:08 - 0:22] CREDENTIAL

For context. I started the YouTube channel in September 2015. My own published trajectory is $1,000 per month, through a $60 per week hardship period, to $23,000 per month current income, over 3 years. The audience on the channel is now 590,000 subscribers. The income lane is paper-asset positions, side-income lines, and the consistent flip-invest-reinvest cycle the channel is built on. Not investment advice.

[0:22 - 0:38] THE GAP

Most 9-to-5 viewers I talk to have two endpoints in their plan. Watch the YouTube content for the trajectory and the inspiration at the top. Hope a side hustle takes off at the bottom. There is no middle. No structured way to run the income audit, stabilise the cash flow, build the first $1,000 saved-and-protected, open the brokerage account, or scope the first side-income line in an order that actually compounds.

[0:38 - 0:55] THE COHORT

I built a cohort to fill that middle. Two tracks. Track A for the 9-to-5 viewer at zero passive-income revenue, 8 weeks, deep. Track B for the operator with one income line already earning, 6 weeks, tactical. Both run with me on weekly live calls, office hours, and a cohort room you keep beyond graduation. Caps at 25 seats on Track A, 30 on Track B. Four times a year.

[0:55 - 1:10] OUTCOMES

By the end of Track A you have an income audit complete, the first $1,000 saved-and-protected, a brokerage account opened, the first dividend or index-fund position in (general category, not specific pick), and the first side-income line scoped and launched. By the end of Track B you have a second income line in build, a portfolio allocation across general asset categories, tax-efficiency structure mapped, and a plateau intervention installed.

[1:10 - 1:25] PRICING + COMPLIANCE

Pricing sits between the $249 Simple Budget Course at the bottom of the ladder and 1:1 work at the top. The cohort teaches general financial education and operator coaching. Not investment advice. Results vary by capital, market, and operator execution.

[1:25 - 1:30] CTA

If you have watched the videos, this is the install version. Application is on the page below. Apply, and I will see you on week one.

B-roll cuts (optional, can be all talking-head for v1)

  • Tommy at desk with laptop and brokerage app open (5 seconds, cut in at 0:25 - blur or out-of-focus screen, no specific positions visible)
  • Income audit spreadsheet on a screen being scrolled (3 seconds, cut in at 0:42 - generic numbers, anonymised)
  • Cohort room screenshot - Circle or Slack (5 seconds, cut in at 0:43)
  • A short cut of Tommy at a book stack referencing the "Read a Book a Week" pillar at 0:08 (3 seconds, brand-anchor)

If shooting tight, skip b-roll. Talking-head is enough at this length.

Captioning

Burn-in captions required. Most viewers watch on mobile with sound off in the first 3-5 seconds of the landing-page autoplay. Caption styling: clean sans-serif, white text with subtle drop shadow, positioned in the lower third above any lower-third graphic. The compliance line at 1:10 to 1:24 needs to be legible on mobile - high-contrast captioning, no animation distractions on that segment.

Open and close frames

Open: First frame is Tommy sitting calmly, hands visible, mid-sentence. Avoid a wide intro shot or a logo card - the first 1.5 seconds should be voice + face only to hold the autoplay viewer.

Close: Final frame is Tommy holding eye contact with the camera at "see you on week one." Cut to the page CTA below without a logo bumper. Logo-bumpers cost 2-3 seconds of attention at the moment the viewer is closest to clicking.

Where this VSL lives

  • Primary: Hero video on the cohort landing page (landing-page.html)
  • Secondary: Pinned video on Tommy's YouTube channel + cross-posted as IG Reel + TikTok
  • Tertiary: First end-screen card on the next 10 YouTube long-forms (with the landing-page URL captioned)

Demo version for the hub

For the demo / hub version of the landing page, the production VSL has not been filmed yet. The hero on the demo uses a placeholder card with the visual treatment: dark hero panel, "A Word From Tommy" eyebrow, a pull-quote anchored to a publicly-verifiable line from his own course landing (the trajectory framing), and a note that the cohort-intro VSL embeds on Day 1 of install.

Day 1 of the install swaps that placeholder card for either:

  • The freshly-filmed cohort VSL above, or
  • An embed of his single highest-watched YouTube long-form (one of the trajectory-and-net-worth pillars) via the standard YouTube embed protocol

Compliance line in the VSL

The script line "Not investment advice. Results vary by capital, market, and operator execution" plus the on-screen overlay at 1:10 to 1:24 is non-negotiable. It draws the regulated-niche line for income-claims compliance in the personal-finance / make-money-online category. Do not cut it in editing. The full compliance line in the script also includes the phrase "general financial education and operator coaching" to anchor the cohort positioning away from regulated investment advice. Both lines ship in the cut.

What this VSL is not

  • Not a long-form sales letter (the long-form ships after first cohort proves conversion)
  • Not a stock-picking video (the cohort is general financial education, not personalised investment advice)
  • Not a story-arc origin video (origin lives in the email sequence, not the landing hero)
  • Not a "what is passive income" explainer (the audience is past that, they are watching for the install version of the playbook)
  • Not a Lambo / mansion / private-jet B-roll display - the visual matches Tommy's actual content register
