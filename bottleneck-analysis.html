Bottleneck Analysis - Tommy Bryson

Built: 2026-05-16 Frame: Cold first-touch. No prior outreach. This is the structural map - the four things I would build first if I were stepping into the Tommy Bryson funnel today. Scope: The gap between a 590K-subscriber YouTube audience explicitly self-selected for "9-to-5 income to passive income" content and the productised mentorship lane the audience is asking for but cannot currently buy.

The one-sentence diagnosis

The Tommy Bryson ladder is essentially one step deep - free YouTube content at the top, a $249 budget course in the middle, a £2.50 / month Patreon tier at the bottom, with the productised mentorship lane the 590K-subscriber audience is asking for sitting empty. The cohort is the wedge.

The four things I would build first

One. The mentorship cohort layer between free YouTube content and the $249 budget course is the missing revenue line.

The current funnel offers three endpoints. Free YouTube content for the audience that wants the "9-to-5 to passive income" answer at the top of the funnel (zero dollars). A $249 entry-level budgeting course in the middle (Long Term Team brand on Teachable, framed as the cash-flow foundation, with three video testimonials and one named-customer text testimonial on the landing). A £2.50 / month Patreon tier at the bottom (currently sitting at 59 patrons, which is unusually low for a 590K-subscriber YouTube audience).

Everyone in the 590K audience who has watched the trajectory videos and wants the full playbook applied to their own income stage has nowhere to land. The free call hook exists, but it has no productised next step behind it - the call is the end of the funnel, not the gateway to a paid mentorship lane.

A productised mid-tier closes the gap. Two parallel cohorts, both taught live by Tommy:

  • The Long Term Mentorship - Track A (Build The First Passive Income Line). 8 weeks. For the 9-to-5 viewer at zero passive-income revenue. Modules: income audit + cash-flow stabilisation, first $1K to $5K saved-and-protected, the first dividend / index-fund position, the first side-income line (digital product / rental / cash-flowing skill), the first "income from money" milestone, accountability framework. Caps at 25. Quarterly cadence. Mid-four-figure range.

  • The Long Term Mentorship - Track B (Stack The Income Lines). 6 weeks. For early operators with one income line already earning. Modules: line-two and line-three selection, portfolio construction across paper assets and cash-flow assets, tax-efficiency basics for the multi-line operator, the "$5K / mo to $15K / mo" plateau and how to break it. Caps at 30. Lower-four-figure range. (General education, not personalised investment or tax advice.)

Year-1 floor (cohort revenue only):

  • Track A: 4 cycles x 25 seats x $2,000 = $200,000
  • Track B: 4 cycles x 30 seats x $1,200 = $144,000
  • Combined direct: $344,000

That is on top of existing YouTube AdSense, the $249 course line, and Patreon. The conservatism is deliberate: prices are floor numbers, the fill rate assumed is 80% of cap, and the comparable creator cohorts in the personal-finance niche (Graham Stephan workshops, Codie Sanchez cohort tiers, Ramit Sethi flagship live programs) price the operator-track equivalent at $1.5K-$5K. The floor reads understated on purpose. Not investment advice. Results vary by capital, market, and operator execution.

Two. The free call hook is the cohort application surface, hidden inside the link tree.

linktr.ee/tommybryson surfaces a "schedule a free call" hook. That hook is currently the deepest point of the funnel - and it is structurally the right surface to productise as the cohort application gate. Right now the call has no curriculum behind it, no readiness filter in front of it, no application form sized for an actual cohort cap, and no public landing page that frames what the next step is.

The fix is short. Repurpose the free-call hook as the cohort-application surface:

  • Application form (Typeform, uTtFirS4). Short application - which track, current income stage, current liquid savings band, current passive-income revenue band, why now. Manual review on submit. Approval triggers the cohort enrolment link.
  • Cohort landing page sits between the free YouTube content and the application. Frames the two tracks, the trajectory story, the work, the FAQ. Replaces the "schedule a call" link in bio.
  • The free call stays available as the post-application read-out for approved applicants who want to talk before paying. It moves from "the funnel hook" to "the consult step inside the application flow."

Year-1 floor on the application-driven cohort routing:

  • Cohort fills sourced from the application surface (the high-intent slice of the YouTube audience that was already pulling the "free call" link): $40K-$80K Year-1 incremental routing lift
  • The free call inbound stays open, but the inbound volume now pre-qualifies via the application
  • Combined incremental routing floor: $40K-$80K Year-1

The free call hook is doing the application's job today without the form, the cap, or the curriculum behind it. The fix is 2-3 days of install work plus a written cohort curriculum.

Three. No email list, no scorecard, no lead magnet - the 590K YouTube audience exits the funnel untracked.

There is no visible Tommy-owned email list. The Linktree routes warm traffic to Teachable, Patreon, the free call hook, the merch store, and the Spotify podcast - none of which capture an email into a tracked, segmented list owned by the creator. There is no quiz, no scorecard, no PDF download, no calculator on any public surface.

Every YouTube subscriber who watches a long-form, gets pulled in by the trajectory framing, and lands on the bio link exits the funnel without entering a list that can be nurtured back into the cohort.

The fix is a single converting asset that does double duty:

"How Close Are You To Your First Passive Income Line?" - a 90-second readiness scorecard. 12 questions across four dimensions: cash flow (income audit clarity, savings rate, debt-to-income), foundation (emergency fund, first $1K saved-and-protected, brokerage account opened), first line (whether the first passive-income line is identified, in-build, or earning), and operator inputs (hours per week available, capital available, accountability stack). Returns a 0-100 readiness score, top-three-gaps email follow-up. Captures email, segments by score band and by current passive-income revenue band.

Conservative IG / YouTube to email conversion on a "9-to-5 to passive income" audience is 4-7% inside a 90-day window across YouTube end-screens, IG bio routing, and the existing free-call traffic. At a combined 590K YouTube + 24K IG distribution and the explicit positioning, a 90-day Year-1 capture range is 8K-20K incremental email subscribers - segmented and tagged at the moment of capture.

That list is the asset that turns every future cohort cycle from "depends on YouTube algorithm" to "open the list, nurture, fill." Not investment advice. Results vary by capital, market, and operator execution.

Year-1 floor on the email asset:

  • Cohort fills sourced from the list (compounds each cycle): $15K-$45K direct lift over the year
  • $249 course sales from list-nurture sequences: $5K-$15K direct lift
  • Combined incremental email-asset floor: $20K-$60K Year-1

Email economics are the part that compounds. Every YouTube long-form that ships into a warm list converts at a multiple of cold. This is not a one-time lift.

Four. The Patreon tier is configured as a tip jar instead of as a community asset.

patreon.com/tommybryson is live at the £2.50 / month tier, currently sitting at 59 patrons. The page revenue is small enough that it reads as a forgotten side surface rather than a load-bearing community lane.

The fix is to reposition Patreon as the alumni / community access layer for the cohort, plus introduce a paid mid-tier (around £15 / mo) for non-cohort viewers who want deeper content access without the cohort commitment. This stops the Patreon page from looking like the back of the funnel, and turns it into the community line that sits next to (not below) the cohort.

This is the slowest lever in the package - it does not move Year-1 revenue the way the cohort or the email asset does. It is on the list because:

  • The current Patreon page is one of the first things a YouTube subscriber sees when they pull up the link tree, and the £2.50 / 59-patron line reads as a signal of dormant monetisation to anyone who notices
  • The Patreon page is the natural home for the alumni community after cohort graduates roll into it
  • A £15 / mo paid mid-tier is the natural "I am not ready for the cohort yet but I want more than the free content" tier

Year-1 floor on the Patreon repositioning:

  • £15 / mo tier filling at 50-150 incremental patrons in the first 12 months: £9K-£27K / yr (~$11K-$34K)
  • Existing £2.50 / mo tier stays as-is and absorbs a slice of cohort grads at no incremental cost
  • Combined incremental Patreon floor: $5K-$15K Year-1

This is the lever that does not move quickly but cleans up the optics of the back end of the funnel and gives the audience a paid step that does not require the cohort commitment.

What does not need to change

  • The YouTube long-form cadence and the trajectory-and-net-worth video format
  • The IG content rhythm and short-form repurposing flow
  • The $249 budget course on Teachable - it stays as the entry-level cash-flow foundation
  • The "Read a Book a Week" pillar - it stays as the brand-credibility anchor
  • The free call hook - it stays alive as the post-application consult step for approved cohort applicants
  • The Tommy Bryson public framing - "9-to-5 income to passive income" - that is the cohort positioning, in his own voice, already public
  • The trajectory story ($1K / mo through $60 / wk to $23K / mo over 3 years, his own number from the course landing) - that is the cohort credibility anchor

What I would install in less than 7 days from kickoff

  1. The Long Term Mentorship landing page (Track A + Track B side by side) deployed with Typeform application form (uTtFirS4) wired to webhook
  2. "How Close Are You To Your First Passive Income Line?" scorecard quiz live on its own subdomain, integrated to the email list
  3. Linktree updated: cohort landing replaces the "free call" link as the primary, scorecard as the secondary capture surface
  4. 5-email cold-first-touch nurture sequence built and loaded into the ESP, triggered on scorecard completion
  5. Brand-locked palette across hub + landing once Tommy ships his brand kit (Day 1 of install)
  6. Pixel + GA4 firing on both surfaces with UTM tags off every YouTube end-screen card, every IG bio link, every Facebook post

The slower-lever items - Patreon repositioning, paid mid-tier Patreon launch, owned podcast cadence increase, paid retargeting - are Day-30 plus, not Day-7. They are not on the install clock.

Deliverable language ("a written income audit complete, a first $1K saved-and-protected line, a first dividend position opened in a brokerage account, a written 12-month execution plan") replaces income-claim language across every surface.
